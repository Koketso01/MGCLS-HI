Welcome to MGCLS–H I (DR1)

The MGCLS–H I database is the dedicated neutral hydrogen (H I) companion to the MeerKAT Galaxy Cluster Legacy Survey (MGCLS) , bringing together a homogeneous view of H I in galaxies from groups to massive clusters. In this first release, the database stages {{ hi_stats.num_clusters }} H I cluster fields and {{ hi_stats.num_galaxies }} H I-detected galaxies across the redshift range 0 < z ≲ {{ hi_stats.z_max }}.

All fields are processed through one consistent end-to-end workflow, with spectral-line calibration and imaging carried out with CARACal , source finding performed with SoFiA-2 , and completeness testing supported by 3D-Barolo . This uniform processing helps avoid the mixed sensitivities, resolutions and selection effects that often complicate comparisons across legacy H I surveys.

The released data provide a uniform interferometric view of H I over {{ hi_stats.v_min }}–{{ hi_stats.v_max }} km/s, corresponding to approximately {{ hi_stats.f_min }}–{{ hi_stats.f_max }} MHz, with typical image rms values of {{ hi_stats.rms_min_mjy }}–{{ hi_stats.rms_max_mjy }} mJy/beam, column-density sensitivities reaching about {{ hi_stats.nhi_min_html|safe }}–{{ hi_stats.nhi_max_html|safe }} cm⁻², angular resolutions of {{ hi_stats.beam_min }}–{{ hi_stats.beam_max }} arcsec, and dynamical cluster halo masses spanning {{ hi_stats.mass_min_html|safe }}–{{ hi_stats.mass_max_html|safe }} M☉.

The database is designed for fast scientific use, whether the goal is to study gas stripping, environmental quenching, scaling relations, or to benchmark simulations. This release includes science-ready H I source catalogues, cluster-level moment maps and velocity fields, per-galaxy H I cubelets, signal-to-noise and mask products, and multi-wavelength preview figures. Hosted at Rhodes University with support from NRF–SARAO and partner institutions, the site is intended as a clean, browsable and downloadable H I layer for the broader MGCLS legacy.
